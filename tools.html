TFSA contribution room

Your room starts the year you turned 18 (or became a Canadian resident), but never earlier than 2009. The CRA My Account shows your official figure.

Withdrawals add back to your room only in the following calendar year. Exceeding your room costs 1% per month on the excess, so check the CRA figure before a large contribution.

Capital gains tax

In 2026 one half of a capital gain is taxable. The proposed increase to two thirds was cancelled.

The sale of your principal residence is generally not taxable, and other exemptions may apply. Confirm your situation with us before you sell.
